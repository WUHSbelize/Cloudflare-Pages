WELCOME TO WUHS

Your Medical Education. Your Future.

Washington University of Health and Science (WUHS Belize) is a medical education institution in San Pedro, Belize, focused on preparing students for the challenges and responsibilities of modern healthcare.

Our academic pathway brings together foundational learning, pre-clinical sciences and clinical sciences to help students progressively develop the knowledge, skills and professional qualities required for a career in medicine.

At WUHS, students are encouraged to learn, collaborate, develop professionally and build the foundation for their future in healthcare.
